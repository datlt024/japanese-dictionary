## General principles

Grammar data must prioritize:

* correctness
* consistency
* beginner readability
* Vietnamese naturalness

Avoid machine-translated explanations.

Avoid mixing multiple styles.

---

## Grammar notation

Always use:

* V
* A-い
* A-な
* N

Do not use:

* Verb
* Noun
* i-adjective
* na-adjective

---

## Formation rules

Examples:

Good:

```text id="vnhk6t"
Vます bỏ ます + 始める
A-な + に
N + の + ようだ
```

Avoid:

```text id="h3mxfa"
Verb stem + 始める
i-adjective + ...
```

---

## Removed endings

Use Japanese notation.

Examples:

```text id="lc6j89"
Vます bỏ ます
A-な bỏ な
Vて
Vた
```

Avoid English grammar terminology.

---

## Common pairs

Frequently co-occurring expressions should be preserved.

Examples:

```text id="cnnkn9"
もし〜たら
つい〜てしまう
まるで〜ようだ
たとえ〜ても
```

These expressions are not separate grammar points.

They are common usage patterns.

---

## Similar grammar

Only compare grammar with genuinely similar meaning or usage.

Good:

* みたい vs ようだ
* らしい vs ようだ
* そうだ vs ようだ
* てしまう vs ちゃう

Avoid meaningless comparisons.

Bad:

* てしまう vs ておく
* から vs ようだ

---

## Difference explanations

Focus on:

* nuance
* register
* natural usage
* speaker intention

Avoid repeating the same meaning.

---

## Explanation style

Vietnamese explanations should be:

* natural
* concise
* easy to understand

Avoid literal translations from English.

Avoid overly academic wording.

---

## Example sentences

Examples should:

* sound natural
* be useful for learners
* avoid unnecessary complexity

Prefer daily-life situations.

---

## Japanese content

Japanese examples must remain unchanged.

Do not rewrite natural Japanese unnecessarily.

---

## What is NOT grammar

Do not treat normal vocabulary as grammar.

Examples:

```text id="szt2u8"
どう
どこ
いつ
だれ
何曜日
どのぐらい
```

These are vocabulary items, not grammar points.

---

## Variants

Pattern variants belong in variant tables.

Examples:

```text id="f0c1t0"
〜てしまう
〜ちゃう
```

They are related forms of the same grammar.

---

## Short forms

Short forms should preserve meaning.

Do not create artificial abbreviations.

---

## Vietnamese language

All explanations must be written in Vietnamese.

Avoid mixing English inside explanations.

English may exist only as fallback data.

---

## Existing database

Prefer existing database values.

Do not overwrite manually curated content unless explicitly requested.

Preserve consistency across grammar records.
